The gate, stated plainly

Almost every posting at $200,000 and up sets a minimum number of years in a named role at an employer, such as "8+ years of software engineering" or years managing engineering teams. Screening software and recruiters check that first. Independent work is often counted as zero years. The resume shows no dates, by your standing rule, which keeps age out of it but also gives the screener nothing to count. LinkedIn already shows dates on positions. Two things move the gate: one outside client engagement delivered and documented, and a referral into a company, which skips the screen.

Supervisor and trainer roles are listed across several employers and your own businesses, as you described them. No carrier, headcount or length is claimed.

Cover letter: Supabase, Head of AI Native Operations

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for Head of AI Native Operations. The posting asks for someone who has built agents that ran in production, designed the controls that made them safe, and tracks workflows automated, eval pass rates and cost per run. Most companies split that work across AI operations, AI platform and cost, forward deployed engineering, adoption and governance roles. I do all of it for my businesses, as one job I call Intelligence Manager.

My production customer-service agent answers customers on a live site with Stripe checkout. It quotes prices only through tool calls against the price book, every reply passes guards before the customer sees it, and any case it cannot close goes to a person with a reason code and the transcript. It runs under hard limits on output tokens, chat history, steps per run and daily calls, with a switch that keeps the agent off until it is turned on. On a 71-question test I cut its wrong answers from 3 to 0, and found that 21 of the 23 cases it still handed off were policy the business had not decided. Those went to the owner as decisions.

I track cost the same way. Across 58 days I ran 164,039 model calls in 1,416 agent sessions on one flat-rate plan that cost about $380 for the period, with 96.5% of input tokens served from the prompt cache. The same work would have been billed at about $17,000 at list API prices, or about $142,500 without the cache. Before a release, an evaluation gate fails the build when output gets worse, and a 103-scenario live evaluation holds an answer-rate floor that is never lowered without a written reason.

I also run a team of AI agents in parallel under shared written rules and a usage budget. I would welcome a conversation.
