Cover letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Forward Deployed Engineer role on the Agentforce team (JR349466) and for the Agentforce Orchestration FDE role (JR357427). I live in Washington and can work remote or from the Bellevue office, with the travel the roles require.

Your postings describe agents that take real actions with humans in the loop, tracked against customer KPIs. I build that kind of system today. My production customer-service agent runs on a live small-business site with Stripe checkout. It answers questions from the business’s knowledge base and quotes prices only through tool calls against the price book. Every reply passes guards before the customer sees it, and any case the agent cannot close goes to a person with the reason and the transcript. When a customer asks about a policy the business has not decided, the retrieval tool returns no answer and a person replies.

Before a release, I test changes with an evaluation gate that fails the build when LLM or RAG output gets worse. My other public builds are an agent harness with cost and step ceilings and a failure taxonomy, and an LLM gateway with cross-provider failover. The gate, the harness and the gateway are public on GitHub, and I can walk you through the production agent in detail.

I also work full time inside a freight operation, which is the kind of supply chain and logistics account the Orchestration team serves. I would welcome the chance to talk about either role.
